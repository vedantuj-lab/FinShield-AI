# PART 1 — PROJECT IDENTITY AND VISION

## 1.1 Project Name

FINSHIELD AI

## 1.2 Tagline

"Plan Smarter. Invest Wiser. Detect Fraud. Protect Your Financial Future."

## 1.3 Project Theme

AI FOR FINANCE

## 1.4 Project Overview

Build FINSHIELD AI, a comprehensive AI-powered financial intelligence and protection platform designed primarily for individuals, investors, traders and small businesses in India.

Finance involves multiple interconnected activities, including managing expenses, budgeting, investing, understanding markets, calculating taxes, detecting fraud and managing business finances.

However, people frequently use separate applications for each activity. They may use one application to track expenses, another to understand investments, another to calculate taxes and another to identify suspicious payment requests.

FINSHIELD AI aims to bring these financial activities into one unified platform.

The application must use AI, financial calculations, data analysis, explainable risk assessment and automated workflows to help users make more informed financial decisions.

It must cover all six directions of the AI for Finance theme:

1. Smart Budgeting.
2. Investing and Portfolio Guidance.
3. Trading and Market Insights.
4. Tax Assistance.
5. Scam and Fraud Detection.
6. Small Business Finance.

The application must also provide one unified AI Financial Assistant that connects all six modules.

# PART 10 — MODULE FIVE: SCAM AND FRAUD DETECTOR

## 10.1 Objective

Build an evidence-centred financial scam prevention and investigation system. Analyse the context of a financial interaction rather than simply flagging individual suspicious words.

## 10.2 Multimodal Evidence Analysis

Accept supported evidence types:
- SMS text, WhatsApp text, Screenshots
- Bank notifications, Payment request screenshots
- Suspicious URLs, Call transcripts
- Audio recordings through speech-to-text service

Use OCR for image text extraction. Use speech-to-text for supported audio files.

## 10.3 Social Engineering Detection

Detect and explain indicators:
- Urgency, fear, authority impersonation, secrecy, threats
- Fake KYC requests, fake refunds, suspicious investment promises
- Fake customer-care instructions
- Requests for OTPs, UPI PINs, suspicious payment demands
- Phishing links

Do not automatically classify every urgent message as fraud. Consider the entire context.

## 10.4 Scam Story Reconstruction

Allow users to upload multiple pieces of evidence from one suspected scam. The system must:
- Connect related evidence
- Build a chronological timeline
- Identify relationships
- Display supporting evidence
- Highlight missing information
- Identify conflicting information
- Avoid inventing events or relationships

Provide an investigation graph and timeline.

## 10.5 Explainable Risk Assessment

Display: LOW, MODERATE, HIGH, CRITICAL.

Use a transparent combination of deterministic indicators and AI contextual analysis.

Possible prototype indicators:
- Authority impersonation
- Urgent payment demand
- OTP/PIN request
- Suspicious external link
- Threatening language
- Inconsistent payment purpose

Explain why the risk level was assigned.

## 10.6 Pre-Payment Safety Checkpoint

Allow users to enter:
- Payment amount and recipient familiarity
- Payment purpose
- Who requested the payment
- Communication method and urgency
- Whether independent verification was completed
- Whether confidential information was requested

The AI must identify warning signs and recommend appropriate independent verification.

## 10.7 FINSHIELD RESCUE

Create a structured emergency assistance workflow. Collect:
- Incident date and time
- Amount involved and payment method
- Transaction reference
- Bank or payment provider
- Description and evidence files
- Actions already taken

Never request OTPs, UPI PINs, passwords or CVVs.

Provide guidance to:
- Contact the bank through official channels
- Call India's cybercrime helpline 1930
- Use the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal
- Preserve screenshots and transaction records

Create a downloadable incident report containing:
- Incident summary and timeline
- Transaction details and evidence index
- Actions taken and pending actions
